Organize it your way

Flat and fast, or in folders — you choose. Nothing to sync, nothing to configure, nothing to pay for.

Folders

Create, rename and delete folders; move a note to any of them in one tap.

Instant search

Type and the list filters as you go — titles and content, across all folders.

Pins & colors

Pin what matters to the top and mark notes with a color label to find them at a glance.

Sort your way

By modified, created or title, ascending or descending — set once, applied everywhere.

Task lists

Checkboxes render in the preview and toggle with a tap — your notes double as to-do lists.

Export & share

Share any note as .md or export everything as a zip archive, straight from the app.

Autosave

Every keystroke is saved for you. Close the app mid-sentence — the note is already on disk.

Writing stats

Words, characters, lines and reading time, always in view at the bottom of the editor.

How it works

No onboarding, no sync setup, no first-run maze.

01

Open and write

The first screen is the editor. Tap, type, format — autosave handles the rest.

02

Find it later

Search filters as you type; folders, pins and colors keep the list tidy as it grows.

03

Take it with you

Share a note as markdown or export everything as a zip when you want a copy.

Your notes never leave your headset

Notes works fully offline: no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no cloud, no network permission. Every note is a plain .md file on your device that you can export at any time. Read the full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
