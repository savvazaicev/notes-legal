Notes Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-06

This privacy policy applies to the Notes app (hereby referred to as "Application") for Meta Quest devices that was created by Savva (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. It does not collect, sell, or share any personal information. All of your data is stored locally on your device only and never transmitted to the Service Provider or any third party:

  • Your notes — plain markdown (.md) files you create and edit
  • Your note folders and the metadata associated with each note (pinned state, color label, folder placement)
  • Your app settings (theme, font size, sort order, new-note template, and other preferences)

The Application works fully offline. It does not make any network requests, does not gather precise information about the location of your VR device, does not access your contacts, camera, or microphone, and does not use advertising identifiers.

Sharing and exporting

You can share individual notes or export all of your notes as a compressed archive through the standard system share options. When you do, the content you choose is handed over by the operating system to the app or service you select (for example, a messaging or file-transfer app). The Service Provider is not involved in or able to see what you share or where you send it; the privacy practices of the receiving app or service apply to that data.

Optional Meta telemetry

The Application uses the Meta Spatial SDK. Meta may offer optional, opt-in diagnostics/telemetry for Meta SDKs; this is governed by Meta's own privacy policy and terms (see Meta Privacy Policy). Telemetry is disabled unless you opt in through the system or SDK prompts, and opting in is not required to use the Application. The Service Provider does not receive, access, or review any telemetry data.

Permissions

The Application requests no runtime permissions. It stores and reads your notes, folders, and settings in its own private app storage on your device.

Third Party Access

The Service Provider does not share, sell, or transmit any of your information to third parties. The Application makes no outbound network requests at all; the only way your note content leaves the device is when you explicitly share or export it to an app or service of your choosing (see above), or via the optional Meta SDK telemetry handled entirely by Meta, and only if you opt in.

The Service Provider may disclose information:

  • as required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena, or similar legal process;
  • when they believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect their rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request.

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all collection of information by the Application easily by uninstalling it. You may use the standard uninstall processes as may be available as part of your VR device or via the VR application marketplace or network. Uninstalling the Application permanently removes your notes, folders, and settings from the device. If you opted in to Meta SDK telemetry, you can disable it in the Meta system settings; the Service Provider does not control or receive that data.

Data Retention Policy

All Application data (notes, folders, metadata, settings) is stored only on your device and is retained only as long as the Application is installed. The Service Provider has no server-side copies of your data and cannot access your data. If you delete the Application, your local data is deleted with it. To keep your notes before deleting the Application, use the export option to save a compressed archive.

Children

The Service Provider does not use the Application to knowingly solicit data from or market to children under the age of 13.

The Application does not address anyone under the age of 13. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. Because the Application does not collect any personal information, the Service Provider has no such data to delete; if you believe a child has used the Application, please contact the Service Provider at oisurka@gmail.com so that they can take any necessary actions.

Security

The Service Provider is concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. Because the Application stores your data only on your device and transmits nothing off-device except what you explicitly share or export, there is no Service Provider-side storage to secure. The Service Provider provides physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards for any information they process or maintain.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time for any reason. The Service Provider will notify you of any changes to the Privacy Policy by updating this page with the new Privacy Policy. You are advised to consult this Privacy Policy regularly for any changes, as continued use is deemed approval of all changes.
